# Specification Criteria

The invocation supplies `MECHANICALLY_PROJECTED_SPECIFICATION_DRAFT_CONTRACT:` with the exact closed draft or repair schema, byte bounds, profile semantics, relationship namespaces, revision rules, standards statuses, and SUMMARY shape. It controls. In V3 drafts, assign a stable bounded `draft_key` to every semantic row and keep it unchanged across corrections. Use `objective_keys`, `requirement_keys`, `standard_key`, and typed `{kind,draft_key}` replacement refs; never renumber semantic relationships after insertion or deletion. Source relationships alone remain immutable manifest ordinals. Draft keys are authoring identities, not host canonical item IDs; the host assigns final IDs and lineage.

The invocation also supplies `MECHANICALLY_PROJECTED_SPECIFICATION_LINEAGE_CONTRACT:`. It distinguishes product fields from the separate host-owned frame reference. A repair must not add or promise unsupported frame fields in `source_lineage`; preserve the actual semantic requirements without inventing host provenance.

Produce useful prose and a complete core covering title, summary, audiences, scope, objectives, requirements, acceptance criteria, constraints, non-goals, assumptions, risks, open questions, standards status, exact source coverage, disposition, planning readiness, and confidence.

- Use the selected profile's exact mechanically projected `required_requirement_categories` and `required_standard_categories`.
- Because `requirement.category` is scalar, include at least one requirement for every required requirement category; combining several concerns in one statement does not satisfy an absent category.
- During repair, preserve every already-present required category while adding each missing category; never fix one absence by removing or renaming another required category.
- Give every requirement at least one linked observable acceptance criterion.
- Distinguish what this specification actually supplies from work required of a future implementation. Do not claim complete schemas, verified behavior or closed interfaces merely because a requirement tells an implementer to create them. If the request or your readiness claim requires normative field-level contracts now, supply every referenced structure consistently; otherwise describe the actual behavioral contract and its explicit later design obligations without inflating readiness. Do not defer a caller-required deliverable to evade review.
- Distinguish instructions for producing this specification from constraints on the project it describes. A restriction on this phase's conduct does not by itself prohibit a later separately requested planning phase; do not turn it into a permanent project constraint. Preserve any restriction the caller actually makes project-wide. Explain the scoped treatment in source coverage without discarding the source or weakening current action boundaries.
- Preserve caller hard constraints as exact source-linked constraint statements, non-goals as exact linked non-goal statements, excluded scope in `scope.excluded` with linked non-goals, and binding prior-decision effects as exact linked requirement statements. Add refinements separately; never exclude a mandatory source to make coverage pass. If obligations conflict, retain them and explain the blocker rather than silently override caller intent. The host refuses unrepresentable protected content at admission, before authoring; do not truncate or combine distinct statements.
- `source_item_indexes` on objectives, requirements, acceptance criteria, constraints, non-goals, assumptions, risks, open questions, and standards are the sole model-authored source-to-product relation. Give every semantic item its real source indexes.
- Emit exactly one `source_coverage` row for each manifest ordinal with its disposition and rationale. An incorporated source must appear in at least one semantic item's `source_item_indexes`; an excluded or unresolved source must not. Omit compatibility-only draft `product_item_refs`: the host ignores supplied values and deterministically derives the sealed reciprocal product-item index from the item-side links.
- During repair, fix the authoritative item-side links or source dispositions rather than hand-maintaining a reverse reference table. Missing source rows, out-of-range links, incorporated sources with no item link, and excluded/unresolved sources with item links still fail closed.
- For new starts, `support_claims` must give a specific claim/status for every frame question, targeting its request uncertainty ordinal or discovered question ID; standard claims target the draft `standard_key`. Cite only locators in exact admitted slots whose `allowed_uses` name the matching question or standard category. A caller response supports only an actually answered challenged question, not standards; evidence slots support only their admitted uses. A material pending, declined, unavailable, conflicting or unsupported question stays blocking in a `blocked` product with a corresponding open question or unresolved source. Nonmaterial nonblocking questions may remain open in `ready`. A `satisfied_with_evidence` standard needs a particular supported claim; matching category or artifact presence alone is not proof. **Every standard whose status is not `satisfied_with_evidence` must use `evidence_artifact_slots: []`, and every descriptive standard support claim for it must use `support: []`; reserve selectors for a particular supported `satisfied_with_evidence` claim.** Locators address captured exact bytes, never an outside URL or file. Historical drafts retain their historical evidence rules.
- `ready` has no blocking question or unresolved source. `blocked` names at least one.
- A revision retains unchanged stable semantics and explicitly supersedes every changed or removed prior item.
- On repair, convert each material lifecycle finding into a coherent change across the governing requirement, its observable acceptance criterion, and any affected risk or contingency. Where an outcome depends on an observation after an irreversible boundary, define how absent as well as explicit failed observation is bounded and handled; do not let a missing signal wait indefinitely when that would defeat the stated outcome. Preserve exact caller constraints and distinguish an authorized mechanism from prohibited exposure rather than weakening either.

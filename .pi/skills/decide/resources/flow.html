Decide — disabled evaluation candidate flow

Strict machine-readable mirror of DECIDE_FLOW. The candidate separates analysis, a host-admitted evidence gap, decision authorship, sealing, validity verification, quality critique, and completion admission.

No execution, taskification, or internal clarification state exists. Validity verification precedes Carren quality critique; each repair reseals and re-verifies the current product.

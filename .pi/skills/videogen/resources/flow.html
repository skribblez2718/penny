Bounded loops and terminal meaning

  • NARRATION_SCRIPT self-transitions from authoring to the sequential Carren gate; revision stays pre-synthesis. Its repair budget is separate from operator refinement.
  • REFINE stores raw feedback, maps it to stable scene/beat IDs, and routes only to the earliest affected existing phase. Every route reconverges on full validation, full AUTO_QA, and the hard review gate.
  • Any exhaustion edge reaches engine complete with lifecycle_state=EXHAUSTED, met=false, the latest complete candidate, passed checks, unresolved items, and reasons. Only PUBLISH_HANDOFF → complete may yield HANDOFF_READY.

Collapsed uniform seams

  • Escalation: every escalatable agent phase can transition to unknown on UNCERTAIN or needs_clarification. Those repeated edges are omitted; unknown → awaiting_clarification and all legal resume targets are drawn.
  • Abort: every non-final state has the uniform abort edge to error; repeated abort arrows are omitted. The distinct structured operator abort edge is drawn.

Protected-input rechecks

  • Before an external mutation, finalization, or handoff, exact source and canon/schema/theme/voice/current hashes are rechecked. Exact-source drift returns to INGEST; changed material bindings return to STORYBOARD. Approval and dependent artifacts are invalidated before reuse.
  • Known external job IDs reconcile without duplicate submission. Unknown mutation disposition pauses; it never becomes success or an automatic retry.

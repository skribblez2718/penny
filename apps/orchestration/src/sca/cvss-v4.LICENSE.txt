cvss-v4.ts adapts cvss_score.js and metrics.js to strict typed, pure functions.
cvss-v4-data.json contains the unchanged numeric/string data from cvss_lookup.js,
max_composed.js and max_severity.js, reshaped into JSON (joint EQ3/EQ6 keys are
concatenated). These official protocol tables are part of the general formula.
They are not a lookup of application vectors or findings.

The test fixtures retain exact upstream source bytes as .txt and their digests.
There are no runtime package, network, filesystem or reference-fixture dependencies
other than the local cvss-v4-data.json import.

# Writing Genre Index

- [Application and Personal Writing](application-and-personal.md): READ WHEN drafting résumés, professional summaries, cover letters, biographies, or personal statements — verified experience, bounded fit, authentic reflection, and identity preservation.
- [Correspondence](correspondence.md): READ WHEN drafting email, chat, letters, apologies, requests, or service communication — relationship, ask, timing, impact, and commitments.
- [Decision and Executive Writing](decision-writing.md): READ WHEN drafting executive memos, recommendations, or business cases — decisions, evidence, alternatives, tradeoffs, and requested action.
- [Documentation](documentation.md): READ WHEN drafting technical documentation, setup guides, procedures, or reference material — executable precision, prerequisites, verification, and recovery.
- [Education](education.md): READ WHEN drafting lessons, explainers, or notebook-like teaching material — learner model, definitions, examples, misconceptions, and transfer.
- [Policy](policy.md): READ WHEN drafting policies, standards, or institutional procedures — scope, roles, requirements, exceptions, and enforceability.
- [Public Writing](public-writing.md): READ WHEN drafting articles, posts, or public lessons — defensible point of view, examples, structure, and publication integrity.
- [Research](research.md): READ WHEN drafting evidence-linked reports or research prose — bounded claims, citations, uncertainty, and renderer integrity.

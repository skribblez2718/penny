# Policy and Procedure

Write intentional, enforceable institutional language. Define scope and terms that change interpretation. Name accountable roles, requirements, conditions, evidence, review cadence, exceptions, approvals, and enforcement when relevant.

Use MUST, SHALL, SHOULD, and MAY consistently with the governing convention. Keep defined terms stable and repeat them when precision requires it. Separate normative requirements from explanatory guidance and examples.

A requirement should make responsibility, required action, conditions, timing, evidence, and exception authority clear. Formal language, headings, numbered provisions, tables, and repetition are appropriate when they improve traceability. Avoid ceremonial claims about commitment or culture that do not change the rule.

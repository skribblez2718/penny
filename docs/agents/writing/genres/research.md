# Research Writing

Make bounded claims in conversation with identifiable evidence. Tie each consequential statement to the supplied source or data at the level the genre permits. Distinguish reported result, inference, recommendation, speculation, and unknown. Hedge in proportion to design and uncertainty, not by habit.

Preserve disciplinary terminology, citations, source links, quotations, methods, and qualifications. Do not fabricate research gaps, sources, identifiers, datasets, methods, findings, or significance. A citation supports only what its source establishes.

For sealed, engine-backed research products, integrate the writing standard before sealing. Any later semantic revision must return through the existing grounding and review gates. Deterministic rendering may improve navigation but must not delete evidence or rewrite an accepted core. Ordinary research prose does not require that engine contract. In either case, inspect the complete delivered report, not only the prompt or first draft.
